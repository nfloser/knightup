# Learning sources and course design

- [Lichess chess-openings](https://github.com/lichess-org/chess-openings) supplies CC0 opening names and representative PGNs. It does not supply complete teaching, a complete repertoire, or proof of move quality. Curate branches and validate chess legality and engine evaluation before turning them into lessons.
- [Lichess open database](https://database.lichess.org/) supplies CC0 training puzzles and evaluations. Puzzles must retain provenance, be reviewed for their theme and difficulty, and have legal solutions verified.
- The user-supplied *Bobby Fischer Teaches Chess* scan is a reference for a gradual teaching pattern: short explanations followed by progressively varied active recall and review, particularly checkmate and back-rank patterns. The book is not treated as openly licensed merely because a scan is available. Do not redistribute its pages, wording or illustrations in this public repository. Author original explanations and use independently licensed or original positions.

A meaningful course should connect an objective, explanation, guided examples, responses to common choices, independent practice, and spaced review. Label available coverage accurately; a few repertoire lines are not an exhaustive opening course.
